Conformal Selective Acting: Anytime-Valid Risk Control for RLVR-Trained LLMs

Hamed Khosravi, Xiaoming Huo

NeurIPS 2026 (main track)

Paper Code

The problem

A language model trained with verifiable rewards keeps producing answers of uneven quality, and the inputs it meets in deployment are not the ones it was tuned on. A single confidence threshold picked in advance stops being the right threshold, and nothing announces when that happened.

What we do

Verifier feedback on each output updates an anytime-valid test that certifies a confidence threshold. The model acts when its score clears the threshold and abstains otherwise.

What it buys you

Risk stays bounded at every time step, not only at a sample size fixed in advance — so the system can be watched continuously, and stopped or shipped at any moment, without the guarantee quietly expiring.
