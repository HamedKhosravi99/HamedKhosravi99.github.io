I am a Machine Learning Ph.D. student at Georgia Tech, advised by Professor Xiaoming Huo. Before Georgia Tech I completed an M.S. in Industrial Engineering (Data Science) at West Virginia University and a B.S. in Industrial Engineering at Iran University of Science and Technology.

My research asks a deceptively simple question: when should we believe a learning system's own account of how well it is doing? I work on statistically principled methods for evaluating and improving AI systems — sequential decision making under non-stationarity, risk control for LLMs trained with verifiable rewards, and causal design for deciding which evidence is worth buying in the first place. I also spent 2025 at Tesla building production ML pipelines for large-scale modeling and evaluation.

I am always glad to talk about research or possible collaborations — feel free to reach out.

Research Interests

Statistical machine learning · Reinforcement learning · Multi-armed and contextual bandits · Non-stationary and online learning · Statistical evaluation of AI systems · LLM evaluation and risk control · Conformal prediction and uncertainty quantification · Causal inference and experimental design · Sequential decision making
